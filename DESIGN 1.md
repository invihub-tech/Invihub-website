## Brand & Style

The brand conveys deep industrial authority, mechanical precision, and advanced hardware innovation. Built for enterprise hardware engineers, procurement leaders, and supply chain architects, the aesthetic bridges the gap between laboratory instrumentation and high-throughput electronics manufacturing. The UI evokes reliability, surgical precision, and computational power.

The design movement combines **High-Precision Dark Tech** and **Technical Functionalism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal structural foundations (#0B0F17, #111827) mimicking anodized CNC aluminum chassis and darkroom cleanroom environments.
- Electric cyan (#00F0FF) and structural cobalt (#2563EB) signal active telemetry, laser scanning, and computational states.
- High-visibility industrial amber (#F59E0B) indicates physical fabrication states, thermal tolerances, and quality verification warnings.
- Subtle hairline blueprint grids, micro-etched dividers, coordinate markers, and technical badge indicators establish an exacting engineering vernacular.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default dark color space optimized for high-density monitoring displays, CAD-adjacent dashboards, and factory-floor field devices:

- **Primary (`#00F0FF` - Electric Cyan):** Active states, laser-guided paths, high-priority telemetry, telemetry highlights, and digital calibration elements.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` - Cobalt Blue):** Structural controls, primary navigation elements, active structural panels, and verified validation badges.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B` - Industrial Amber):** Tooling warnings, active thermal dissipation alerts, physical machinery cycle notices, and urgent yield indicators.
- **Neutral Core (`#0B0F17` Base Surface, `#111827` Panel Surface, `#1E293B` Border/Elevations):** Multi-tiered slate blacks with neutral-cool undertones that eliminate ambient glare while preserving optical contrast.
- **Supportive Neutrals:** Text and metrics use `#F8FAFC` (High-Contrast White), `#94A3B8` (Muted Technical Metadata), and `#475569` (Inactive Structural Wireframes).

## Typography

The type hierarchy employs three specialized typefaces to cleanly separate thematic messaging, high-density data, and functional instrumentation:

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles (`Space Grotesk`):** Delivers a mechanized, forward-leaning geometric cadence for section headlines, hero titles, and high-level architectural classifications.
- **Body & Editorial Narrative (`Inter`):** Handles technical project descriptions, procurement documents, and high-readability narrative segments with balanced optical kerning.
- **Labels, Telemetry & Schematics (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical data points, tolerances, BOM numbers, manufacturing cycle counters, and structural badges. Always set with precise tracking to emphasize instrument-grade clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an exacting 8-point base grid (with a 4-point sub-grid for technical metadata alignments) designed to mirror engineering schematic drawings.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column fluid layout, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Structural touch targets maintain a minimum dimension of 44x44px.
  - **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Secondary side-rails and data drawers reveal modular telemetry cards.
  - **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column rigid grid with a maximum content boundary of 1440px, 48px margins, and 24px gutters.
- **Technical Blueprint Accents:** Section bounds leverage hairline 1px framing lines with subtle coordinate markers (`[X:01 // Y:88]`) in the outer margins, providing spatial grounding without impeding scanning flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **Tonal Layering**, **Crisp Hairline Outlines**, and **Targeted Photonic Glows** instead of conventional diffuse drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#0B0F17`. Background for top-level viewport frames.
- **Level 1 (Structural Shells & Panels):** `#111827` overlaid with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`. Used for passive manufacturing data cards and side navigations.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Modules & Flyouts):** `#161F30` with a 1px border of `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.2)`. Applied to active machine-status monitors, focused forms, and contextual popovers.
- **Level 3 (Modal Alerts & Overlays):** `#1E293B` featuring a dual border: 1px inner solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` and an outer 1px structural boundary.
- **Photonic Glow (Active States):** High-priority controls emit a tight, directional neon glow: `0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)` or `0 0 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)` for warning states, simulating illuminated hardware interfaces.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Precision Soft-Chiseled** architecture (roundedness: `1`). Micro-radii of 2px to 4px evoke precision-milled CNC parts, clean-cut silicon dies, and durable industrial field enclosures.

- Standard buttons, badges, and input containers employ 4px border radii (`0.25rem`).
- Large structural hardware viewports and telemetry cards scale up to a maximum radius of 8px (`0.5rem`).
- Technical callouts and corner indicators may incorporate chamfered corner accents (45-degree micro-angles) to accentuate structural tooling aesthetics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cybernetic Action:** Background in `#00F0FF`, text in `#0B0F17` (`font-weight: 600`), uppercase `JetBrains Mono` label, 4px border radius. Hover introduces a bright cyan outer flare (`box-shadow: 0 0 14px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Industrial Action:** Transparent base, 1px border in `#2563EB`, text in `#F8FAFC`. Hover fills with `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)` with a brightened `#60A5FA` border.
- **Destructive/Safety Stop:** Transparent base, 1px border in `#EF4444`, text in `#EF4444`. Hover state shifts to `#EF4444` background with `#0B0F17` text.

### Chips & Spec Badges
- **Technical Status Badge:** Inset background `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.8)`, 1px border tinted to its state (Cyan for "ONLINE/PROTOTYPING", Amber for "CALIBRATING/HEAT CYCLE", Green for "QC_PASSED").
- Typography is strictly `label-sm` in `JetBrains Mono`, prefixed with a 6px status LED dot.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Controls:** Background `#0B0F17`, 1px border in `#334155`, text `#F8FAFC`. Active focus transitions border instantly to `#00F0FF` with a zero-delay 1px ambient glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square and hexagonal-profile selectors. Checked state displays a solid `#00F0FF` core with a micro 1px notch pattern.

### Hardware Manufacturing Cards
- **Structure:** Surface `#111827`, hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Top-right corner includes a subtle coordinate marker or machine ID tag (`M-08 // SMT`).
- **Telemetry Readouts:** Multi-layer card headers featuring split titles (`Space Grotesk`) and live batch counts (`JetBrains Mono` numbers).
- **Hover Micro-interaction:** 1px border shifts to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` with an interactive subtle blueprint crosshair icon surfacing in the upper right.

### Specialized Hardware Components
- **Tolerance Range Indicators:** Linear segmented tracks rendering dynamic thermal and dimension tolerances with `#00F0FF` (nominal) and `#F59E0B` (variance limit) markers.
- **BOM Inspection Drawer:** Dense tabular layout utilizing `JetBrains Mono` for rapid SKU, lead-time, and component packaging scanning.
