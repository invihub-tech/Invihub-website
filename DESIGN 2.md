## Brand & Style
The design system reflects high-precision engineering, high-throughput manufacturing, and technological mastery. Its persona is disciplined, uncompromising, and architectural. Visual tension is achieved not through decorative embellishment, but through structural rigor, mathematical alignment, high contrast, and mechanical micro-details.

The emotional signature is that of an advanced cleanroom or an automated aerospace machining cell: immaculate dark environments punctuated by high-visibility cadmium yellow indicators.

Key principles:
- **Mechanical Precision**: Clean lines, 1px structural framing, and strict geometric proportions.
- **Controlled Restraint**: Monochromatic depth hierarchy where color acts purely as an instrument of telemetry and focus.
- **Industrial Minimal**: Elimination of ornamental blur, soft pill contours, or ambient glow in favor of crisp technical surfaces.

## Colors
The system operates on an absolute pure-black (`#000000`) void, establishing maximum dynamic range. Depth is constructed using four discrete tonal surfaces, paired with laser-sharp borders. Industrial yellow is the sole chromatic accent, strictly metered to occupy 3–5% of the total visual area. Cool spectrum tones (blue, purple, cyan) are strictly prohibited.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas / Void**: `#000000` — Absolute base background.
- **Surface-1 (Primary Container)**: `#080808` — Structural card layers and telemetry panels.
- **Surface-2 (Section Break)**: `#101010` — Alternating row groups, toolbars, and grouped content zones.
- **Surface-3 (Elevated Surface)**: `#181818` — Modals, popovers, active overlays, and floating heads-up readouts.
- **Surface-4 (Interactive Highlight)**: `#222222` — Focused rows, table hover states, pressed containers.
- **Stroke Default**: `#292929` — Structural divider, panel boundary.
- **Stroke Hover / Active**: `#3A3A3A` — Hovered control outline, active tab indicator frame.
- **Accent Core (Primary)**: `#F5B82E` — Interactive call-to-actions, crucial sensor thresholds, primary telemetry.
- **Accent Light**: `#FFC94A` — Focused CTA states, highlighted metrics, active tool selectors.
- **Accent Dark**: `#C98D12` — Active press states, industrial secondary indicators.
- **Type Solid**: `#FFFFFF` — Primary headlines and critical real-time values.
- **Type High-Contrast**: `#F5F5F5` — Section titles, table headers.
- **Type Reading**: `#B8B8B8` — Specifications, body paragraphs, logs.
- **Type Muted**: `#707070` — Units of measure, secondary timestamps, disabled controls.

## Typography
Manrope powers the entire typographic architecture. Its geometric backbone delivers technical clarity and functional legibility across numeric data tables, machine status arrays, and executive overviews.

- **Display & Headlines**: Heavy weights (`700` and `800`) paired with negative tracking create a dense, industrial mechanical impact.
- **Labels & Metrology**: All upper-range micro-labels, telemetry headers, and status flags use uppercase letterforms with extended letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to simulate laser-etched equipment plates.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: Numerical data readouts utilize proportional figures with strict alignment.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a modular 8px baseline rhythm with 4px sub-increments for compact telemetry components.

### Grid Parameters
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column grid, max-width 1600px, 24px gutters, 32px safe margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column grid, fluid width, 20px gutters, 24px margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, fluid width, 16px gutters, 16px margins.

### Spatial Discipline
Interfaces prioritize dense information packaging over excessive white space. Cards, metric indicators, and toolsets snap directly into grid boundaries with crisp `1px` structural borders (`#292929`), preventing visual bleed and maintaining industrial alignment.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized entirely through tonal layering and structural outlines. Diffuse shadows, blur backdrops, and artificial dropshadows are eliminated to maintain a stark, functional technical aesthetic.

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#000000`. Background floor.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Enclosures)**: `#080808` background with a solid 1px border (`#292929`).
- **Level 2 (Inlaid Elements & Strips)**: `#101010` inset within Level 1.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Floating & Flyouts)**: `#181818` with a 1px `#3A3A3A` border. Used for modals, dropdown panels, and diagnostics.
- **Level 4 (Hover / Precision Highlight)**: `#222222` with a 1px border (`#F5B82E` on selected components).

## Shapes
Geometry is strictly angular, precise, and utilitarian:
- All cards, containers, input fields, and buttons utilize an architectural `4px` corner radius.
- Large contextual panels, diagnostics frames, and primary display enclosures scale up to an absolute maximum of `6px`.
- Pill shapes, fully rounded capsules, and circular geometries are forbidden, with the sole exception of miniature `6px` status pulse dots.
- Internal borders, dividers, and rule-marks remain `1px` wide with no feathered edges.

- Background `#080808`, 1px border `#292929`, corner radius `4px` or `6px`.
- Interior sections divide with `1px solid #292929`.
- Header bars utilize an upper `#101010` strip with uppercase `label-sm` text in `#707070` accompanied by industrial monospaced code notation.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Input**: Background `#000000`, 1px border `#292929`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `4px`, height `40px`, padding `0 12px`. Focus: border `1px solid #F5B82E`, box-shadow `none`.
- **Checkbox & Radio**: 16x16px box, radius `2px` (radios `2px` chamfered/square, avoiding soft circles). Border `1px solid #3A3A3A`. Checked: background `#F5B82E`, icon `#000000`.

### Chips & Telemetry Badges
- Background `#101010`, border `1px solid #292929`, radius `4px`, padding `4px 8px`.
- Text in `label-sm` (`#B8B8B8`).
- Operational State Accent: A 6x6px solid indicator dot in `#F5B82E` for active status, `#707070` for standby.

### Data Tables & Spec Sheets
- Header row: Background `#080808`, border-bottom `1px solid #292929`, text `label-sm` in `#707070`.
- Cells: Padding `12px 16px`, border-bottom `1px solid #101010`, text `body-md` in `#B8B8B8`.
- Row hover: Background `#101010` with text `#FFFFFF`. Selected row: left accent border `2px solid #F5B82E`.
